Our Story

Manufacturing premium, certified jute & cotton bags from Kolkata, India since 2009 — built for retailers who won't compromise on quality or sustainability.

Since 2009

Trusted Across Europe

Juteberry started in Kolkata — the historic heart of India's jute industry — with a simple aim: make sustainable packaging that European retailers could rely on without compromise. Every bag is REACH compliant, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified, and produced under ISO 9001:2015 and Fairtrade standards.
